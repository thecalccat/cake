About Me

Hi there! My name is Hannah, but most people call me Cake 🩷

I'm a Knoxville native, having graduated from Oak Ridge HS and UTK for my bachelor's. I met my husband Vinny in high school and he my biggest supporter in all I do. He even moved all the way to Nebraska with me for my first job! That's where I ended up getting my start in tattooing back in 2021. After several years, I was finally able to move back to Tennessee in 2025 to be with Vinny while he finishes his PhD at UTK.

For tattooing, while I am versatile enough to tackle almost any style, my true specialties are Color Realism - specifically jewels and moody, classic oil paintings - and bold color Neo-Traditional pieces. I'd love to do more of both!

When I'm not at the studio, you can usually find me at home drawing designs for your next appointment while cuddling with my cat, Dumpling. To unwind, I'm almost always either rewatching It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia - for the hundredth time - or getting lost in a video game like Pokémon or Red Dead Redemption 2.

PS... I am always open to trading tattoo services for rare Pokémon cards. Gotta Catch 'em all, am I right?
